Reading the notes

Whenever the generator can't give you exactly what you asked for, it writes a note on the puzzle instead of quietly giving you less. The analysis pane shows every note, and so does ConveyorData.Notes from code. Each one starts with what it is about, and the Generating page lists them all. ConveyorAudit.MeetsRequest(puzzle) tells you whether a puzzle got everything its settings asked for, ConveyorAudit.Missing(puzzle) lists what it missed, and a pack's Re-roll > Fallbacks replaces the ones that missed.

"Target: 5 stars not found in 40 tries; the closest has 4."

The stars follow the board's size, because a bigger board holds a longer par and more places to get lost. When your target is outside what the board gave, the note says so and which way to change its size. Up to 6 x 6 gives 1 star (2 with ice at 6 x 6), 7 x 7 2, 8 x 8 3, 10 x 10 4, and 12 x 12 and up 5, and every size makes every level below its most. Also:

  • raise Max attempts under Advanced, so more seeds are tried;
  • remember that gems raise the score: it is multiplied by 1 + gems / 4, and gems lengthen the par a lot, so a gem puzzle rates higher than a plain one on the same board. Use a smaller board for a low target with gems;
  • a tight Max par can keep a puzzle below the stars you asked for. Auto raises it when it can never reach them.

"Verified: no puzzle in 40 tries was proven solvable with no dead ends and a par of ..."

No board met the hard requirements: proven solvable with no dead ends, inside your par window, with your chest spots, every switch and gem needed, and the live holes asked for. The note lists what it was looking for, what the closest board has, and what to try: fewer of a gimmick, a bigger board, ice for more chest spots, a higher Max par, more climb steps or a bigger state budget.

Auto fixes the settings known to fight each other, and the window shows what it would change before you press it.

Fewer gimmicks than asked

Every switch and gem has to be needed by the best solution, and every hole has to end some ride of two or more cells, so one that would change nothing isn't kept. Verified generation tries more seeds until a puzzle has them all, and the window's Only on target keeps just the puzzles that got everything. When none of the tries does, the "Verified:" note says how many the closest one has.

  • Switches: two needed switches need room, so fewer fit on small boards, and a low star target can leave no room for a second. Use a bigger board or one switch.
  • Gems: every gem adds moves, so a tight Max par or a low star target can leave room for fewer. Raise Max par, raise the target or use a bigger board.
  • Holes: only an ice slide can carry the piece into a hole, so a board with little ice has room for few. Use a bigger board or fewer holes.

No holes on the board

Holes need Ice. No belt ever points into a hole, so without ice the only way in would be pushing straight into one from next to it, which isn't a puzzle. A board asked for holes without ice gets none, with the note "Builder: no holes: holes need Ice", and Auto turns Ice on.

Few chest spots

On a belts-only board the piece only rests on stop tiles, and most of them are on the best route, so an 8 x 8 board has only about 3 to 7 chest spots of its own. Asking for more works, but each one past that costs about a move of par, and past the stop tiles the board can have, it can't be met (Auto lowers it). Turn on Ice for many more: ice boards have about 16 (8 x 8) to 26 (10 x 10) anyway.

A hand-made board misbehaves

The generator keeps its rules on every board it makes, but a board you build by hand only has to pass ConveyorRules.Validate, which checks the data, not the design:

  • A belt pointing into a wall or off the board stops the piece on that belt. The rules play it correctly, but it looks like a broken belt, which is why the generator never makes one.
  • Belts that pass the piece round in a circle make that move refused ("That move would never end"), and nothing moves.
  • "Can get stuck": a hand-made board can have dead ends. Check CanGetStuck after ConveyorSolver.Verify, and give players Undo and Restart, as the demo does. Its card also says when there is no way to finish from where the player is.

Generation feels slow

Measured on a desktop PC outside Unity, 24 builds of each preset (your machine and Unity's runtime will differ, and a phone is slower):

PuzzleTime for one verified puzzle
Belts presets, 5 x 5 to 14 x 145 to 125 ms
Difficulty presets (a star target)22 to 680 ms
Ice and chest spots10 to 121 ms
Gems12 to 152 ms
Switches26 to 477 ms
Holes on ice27 to 854 ms

A star target that takes several tries is the usual slow case. The demo's Endless recipes, measured the same way, took up to about 2.3 seconds.

  • In the window, generation runs on a background thread with a progress bar that says what the board is doing, and Cancel stops it within a fraction of a second. The Time limit (60 seconds by default) keeps the closest board that finished, with a "Time limit:" note.
  • In a game, use the Async methods, as the demo does for Endless, or make levels ahead of time in the editor and ship them as a pack.
  • On WebGL there are no threads, so the demo makes Endless puzzles on the main thread there, and the game pauses while it does.

"Solver: ..."

The solver searches up to State budget positions (1,000,000 by default). A plain belt board needs one per cell, each gem can double that and switches multiply it by 2 or 4, so even a 32 x 32 board with 3 gems and Rotate switches stays far below the budget. You would only see this note on a hand-made board with many gems or cracked tiles, or with a very small budget. Raise the State budget under Advanced (up to 5,000,000), or pass a bigger budget to ConveyorSolver.Verify and ConveyorRules.Hint.

"Solver: more than 1,000,000,000 best solutions; the count stops there." only means the count is capped.

The demo ignores taps or keys

The demo uses the Input System package. Set Project Settings > Player > Active Input Handling to Input System Package or Both. The hub makes an EventSystem with the Input System's UI module when the scene has none. If your scene already has an EventSystem, its input module has to match your input handling. Presses on the HUD's card and buttons never move the puck.

Pink or dark in a pipeline

  • Pink in a build: the demo makes its materials in code, and a build strips shaders that only code uses. Before every build, ConveyorLookKeeper writes Runtime/Demo/Resources/SimpleConveyorLook.mat with the active pipeline's lit shader, so the build keeps it. If you see pink, check that file exists after a build (deleting it is fine: the next build writes it again).
  • Dark or tinted in HDRP: out of the box HDRP uses physical light units, automatic exposure and sky lighting. ConveyorDemo.HdrpLook (on by default) adds a global volume at priority 10 with a fixed exposure and a gradient sky, and sets the sun in lux, so the demo looks the same as on the other pipelines. Turn it off to let your own volumes and lights take over.

Resetting the demo's progress

The demo saves each pack level's best moves in PlayerPrefs, under SimpleConveyor.Demo.Best.<pack name>.<level>. Call ConveyorPackProgress.Clear(packName, count) to forget a pack, or delete those keys. A pack's progress is filed under its pack name, so renaming a pack starts it fresh.

Seeds and versions

The same settings and seed always give the same puzzle within a version of Simple Conveyor. A future version that improves the generator may change what a seed produces. Saved assets store the puzzle itself, not just its seed, so your saved puzzles and packs never change. Verified generation stores the seed that actually worked, such as level-7-5#1, so copying a puzzle's own seed always rebuilds that exact puzzle.

Console messages

  • "[Simple Conveyor] Documentation page not found": the documentation menu looks for these pages in a folder whose path contains SimpleConveyor and Documentation. Keep the Documentation folder inside the Simple Conveyor folder.
  • "[Simple Conveyor] Generation failed": something went wrong on the window's generation thread. Please send the message with your settings and seed.
  • "[Simple Conveyor] Kept ... in the build for the demo": the build step wrote the look material, which is expected after you switch render pipelines.
  • "[Simple Conveyor] No lit shader found for the active render pipeline": the pipeline asset has no default material, so the demo may draw pink in a build. Give the pipeline asset a default material.
  • "[Simple Conveyor] Exported 12 file(s) from ...": a pack's Export all finished.
  • "Simple Conveyor hub: no picture for ...": a mode card's picture couldn't be drawn. The card still works without it.

Still stuck

Email livingfailuregames@gmail.com with your Unity version and, for a question about one puzzle, its seed and settings. Copy seed and Copy solution in the analysis pane make that easy, and a seed rebuilds the puzzle exactly.
